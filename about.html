About Us

We are Information Systems students at King Abdulaziz University. We built Smart Car Tracker as our project for CPIS 358, Internet Applications & Web Programming.

Our goal is simple: to give every car owner an easy way to keep maintenance records and expenses together, instead of losing them in paper invoices and memory.

For more academic details, visit King Abdulaziz University.
