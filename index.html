About the System

This system aims to solve the difficulty of tracking vehicle health records and periodic expenses by providing an organized web interface for logging invoices, oil changes, and calculation costs.

Most drivers keep paper invoices in the glove box and try to remember when the last oil change was. When the car needs service, or when it is time to sell it, that history is hard to find. Smart Car Tracker keeps it all in one simple website.

Benefits

  • One place: all your vehicles and their service history together.
  • Know your costs: see what your car costs you, with totals worked out for you.
  • Plan ahead: dates and odometer readings show when the next service is due.
  • Better resale: show a buyer a clear maintenance history.

How It Works

  1. Create a free account, or go straight to the tracker.
  2. Register your vehicle with its brand, year, color and plate number.
  3. Add a record after each service, with the date, odometer and cost.
  4. Check the Expense Summary to see everything you have spent.
